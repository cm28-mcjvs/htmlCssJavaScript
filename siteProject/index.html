The Home of EDM

The home of EDM

Electronic Dance Music (EDM) is a broad range of percussive electronic music genres originally made for nightclubs, raves, and festivals. Since its inception EDM has expanded to include a wide range of subgenres. - Wikipedia

What even is EDM?

To put at its simplest, EDM is any type of music that utilises or focuses its sound around being made with electronics. This can range from certain instruments being electronic, or the entire track being made in a DAW (Digital Audio Workspace). Tracks made with electronics can attempt to mimic the sound of real instruments or just make up sounds that real instruments could never make.

Where did it come from?

Electronic music as a whole began around the start of the 20th century, when electronic inventions at the time were experimented with to make the first electronic musical instruments. While these instruments weren't initally sold, it gained public interest and people saw promise in these technologies. Throughout the 20s and 30s, Musicians started using electronic instruments in their orchestras due to their versatility. Most electronic music made at the time was experimental and never really went mainstream.

Around the 50s and 60s, one of the biggest electronic music instruments was created: the Synthesizer. This instrument allowed users to mix piano notes with electronic sounds, boosting the capabilities of electronic music. This led to the rise of electronic music, with artists like the Beach Boys and the Beatles using electronic elements in their music to define their sound. Over time, electronic music has continued to evolve, and with the creation of DAWs (Digital Audio Workstations) and the internet, electronic music has never been bigger.
