Recipe: Cheesy Nachos

Cheesy goodness without a hole in your wallet.

Prep Time: 2 minutes | Bake Time: 30 seconds | Makes: 1 plate

Ingredients

  • ~1/4 bag Tortilla Chips
  • 1 bag Shredded Cheese

Directions

  1. Place chips flat on plate
    • Space chips evenly
    • Leave little overlap
  2. Sprinkle shredded cheese on top of chips
  3. Microwave at low power for around 30 seconds
