## Part 2: Client Questions
### The Business
- What's the main purpose of your company? (services they provide)
- Who are your main competitors that you want to stand out from?

### The Audience
- Why will people need to visit your website?
- What should the customer be able to do on your website?

### The Content
- What features do you think are **absolutely necessary** for your website?
- Do you want your content to be more word-y or more graphics?
- Who will provide content (such as images/videos) for your site?

### The Branding
- Do you have a scalable logo, and how prominant do you want it? (ask for SVG or PNG)
- If not, what should the logo roughly look like?
- What colors do you primarily want on your site? (prefer hex codes)
- Are there any fonts you have in mind for use on your website?

### The Look
- Are there any websites you want to take inspiration from, whether layout or design?
- Is there any specific theme you're going for, like modern or retro?

### The Project
- How soon do you want the project to be done?
- Do you have a budget for this project, and if so, what?
- Will you need someone to host your website on behalf of you?
