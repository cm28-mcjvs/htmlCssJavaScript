Recipe: Chocolate Chip Cookies

The classic recipe, done my own way.

Prep Time: 15 minutes | Bake Time: 18 minutes | Makes: 24 cookies

Ingredients

  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Cup Sugar
  • 1 Cup Flour
  • 2 tbsp Baking Soda
  • 2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 Cup Chocolate Chips
  • 1/2 Cup Milk

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 350
  2. Combine ingredients into bowl & mix
    • Add powders first
    • Mix until uniform then add Eggs
    • Mix for 30 secs then add milk
    • Add chocolate chips
  3. Place in oven for 18 minutes
  4. Let cool for at least 2 minutes
